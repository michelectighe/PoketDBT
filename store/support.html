Pocket DBT Support

Pocket DBT is an offline, DBT-informed reference tool. It does not provide therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, or crisis care.

Need urgent help in Canada?

If you are in immediate danger or need urgent medical support, call 9-1-1. If you are thinking about suicide, call or text 9-8-8, available 24/7.
