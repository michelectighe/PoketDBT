## Name

Pocket DBT

## Subtitle (30 characters maximum)

## Promotional text

A calm, private cheat sheet for finding and using DBT skills when remembering them feels difficult.

## Description

Pocket DBT puts practical Dialectical Behaviour Therapy skill reminders in one calm, easy-to-navigate place.

When you are not sure what skill fits, “Help me choose” starts with what is happening right now. You can also browse concise guides across all four DBT modules:

- Mindfulness
- Distress Tolerance
- Emotion Regulation
- Interpersonal Effectiveness

Pocket DBT includes guided paced breathing, plain-language reminders for commonly taught DBT skills, and quick access to Canadian crisis resources. It works without an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Pocket DBT is an educational reference and practice aid. It does not provide therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, or crisis care, and is not a substitute for professional support or a personal safety plan.

## App Review notes draft

Pocket DBT is a read-only, offline educational reference for DBT-informed coping skills. It has no login, purchases, advertising, analytics, or user-generated content and stores no user or health data. Crisis buttons open the device Phone or Messages app; the app does not place calls or send messages itself. All functionality is available immediately. The app does not claim to provide therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services.
