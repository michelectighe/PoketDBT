Pocket DBT Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

Pocket DBT is designed as a private, offline reference tool. The app does not require an account and does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information or health information.

Information collection

Pocket DBT does not include advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, user accounts, or cloud storage. Your use of the skills and breathing exercises is not recorded by the app.

Phone and text actions

If you choose a crisis-support button, Pocket DBT asks your device to open the Phone or Messages app. Pocket DBT does not place the call, send the message, or receive information about it. Your carrier and the service you contact may process information under their own policies.

External services

The app may display links or contact actions for third-party crisis and emergency services. Pocket DBT is not responsible for the privacy practices or availability of those services.

Children’s privacy

Pocket DBT does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.
